     - **Lines with hedged fuel exposure**, limiting bunker cost pass-through risk.
   - **Avoid:** Ultra-large container ship operators (20K+ TEU) with fixed capacity and high fixed costs; they face structural demand headwinds.

2. **Marine Reinsurance Cost Outlook:**
   - **Expect 15-20% premium increases** in marine P&I reinsurance programs (2H 2024 renewals) because:
     - Carriers will defer vessel maintenance to preserve margins → increased casualty frequency.
     - Crews will be reduced (cost-cutting) → increased human error claims.
     - Blank sailings and irregular scheduling → increased detention/demurrage claims on cargo side.
   - **Mitigation:** Lock in long-tail marine treaty renewals now; avoid spot market exposure to maritime risk in Q2-Q3.

3. **Inflation Assumption Recalibration:**
   - **Current CPI inflation models may over-rely on "transitory" freight cost declines.** The composite inflationary pressure score of 26/100 masks a **oil price shock (100/100) decoupled from freight rates**—this is a stagflationary signal.
   - **For fixed income valuations:** Do not assume shipping rate deflation feeds back into broader goods inflation. Instead, model **margin compression → pricing power restoration in 6-9 months** (once excess capacity is wrung out via lay-ups). This supports a **"V-shaped" inflation recovery** rather than durable disinflation.
   - **Implication for duration:** Short-duration positioning is more defensible than consensus "lower-for-longer" rates view.
