## (4) ACTIONABLE PORTFOLIO IMPLICATION

### **RECOMMENDATION: Recalibrate inflation assumptions & reinsurance exposure**

**The Problem:**
Your fixed income and multi-asset inflation models likely embed a "sticky freight cost inflation" narrative (assumption: supply-constrained shipping → sustained pricing power → passthrough to CPI). The data contradicts this.

**What the data shows:**
- Freight rates are cooling despite a 72/100 geopolitical risk score and 44.8% bunker cost surge
- Demand destruction (FBX14 -9.2%, CN_USEC flat-to-cooling) is dominant over cost pass-through
- Carriers are entering a **margin compression phase**, not a pricing power phase

**Three concrete actions:**

1. **Shipping Credit Exposure:**
   - **Reduce long-duration exposure** to carrier debt (CMA CGM, MSC, Evergreen bonds). Margin compression + weakening demand = refinancing risk into 2025-26. Container shipping leverage ratios will deteriorate if rates remain in 60-80 range while bunker stays elevated.
   - **Upgrade credit quality filters**: Favor carriers with diversified service portfolios and strong balance sheets (e.g., Maersk) over single-service or leveraged operators.
   - Duration: Reduce 5Y+ exposure; shift to 2-3Y maturities to avoid refinancing cliff.

2. **Inflation Assumption Revision:**
   - **Lower freight inflation assumptions** for Q4 2024–Q2 2025 forecasting. Current models assuming 3-5% quarterly freight contribution to CPI inflation are overstated.
   - Bunker spikes are **temporary and not sustained** into rate stickiness once demand softens—revise downward by 40-50 bps on your core PCE/CPI freight weight.
   - This implies: **lower terminal rate expectations** and supports **duration extension in government bond allocation**.

3. **Reinsurance & P&C Cost Outlook:**
   - Elevated geopolitical risk (72/100) + port congestion (68/100) will drive **higher marine claims frequency** (piracy ransoms, hijacking recovery ops, weather delays, port damages) in Q1-Q2 2025.
   - **Reinsurance cost inflation will *outpace* freight rate inflation** because claims are event-driven, not demand-driven. Secure reinsurance agreements now before 1 Jan renewal cycle.
   - Marine & cargo insurance pricing will harden 15-25% in Jan 2025 renewal; this is not captured in current freight market softness.
