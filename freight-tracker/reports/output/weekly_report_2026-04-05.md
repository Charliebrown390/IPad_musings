### **Recommendation: Reduce Shipping Sector Credit Duration; Hedge Inflation Through Reinsurance Cost Expectations**

#### Primary Thesis:
The freight market exhibits **margin compression in a cost-inflation environment**—a toxic combination for shipping operator credit quality. Current rate signals show:

- **Demand weakness on high-volume routes** (trans-Pacific): No pricing power to pass through cost increases
- **Materialized fuel costs**: Already locked in; no relief available
- **Unmaterialized labour & infrastructure costs**: 12-18 month lag before full pass-through; operators will absorb these as EBITDA margin degradation before rates recover

#### Specific Actions:

**1. Fixed Income Portfolio: Reduce Duration on Shipping-Linked Credits**
   - Avoid/underweight high-leverage shipping operator bonds (especially those with per-ship financing debt) with maturities beyond 2025
   - Margin compression typically precedes covenant breaches by 2-3 quarters; current rate weakness + labour risk = elevated probability of covenant pressure by Q2 2025
   - **Target**: Duration reduction on B-rated shipping issuer debt; upgrade to BBB-rated terminal operators/port operators instead (who have pricing power and lower labour exposure)

**2. Reinsurance Cost Outlook: Expect P&I and Cargo Rate Hardening**
   - The geopolitical risk premium (Hormuz, Red Sea adjacency) is *priced into freight rates* but *underpriced in insurance*
   - If Hormuz tensions escalate to chokepoint disruption, reinsurance accumulation risk (multiple exposed vessels in transit simultaneously) would trigger 30-50% premium increases on war risk, H&M, and P&I
   - **Forward positioning**: Increase allocation to reinsurance companies with strong underwriting discipline in war/political risk (non-proportional cat bonds, cyber+political bundles). Expect ceding commission compression as insurers tighten terms in 2025

#### Risk Quantification:
- **Downside scenario**: Hormuz escalation + labour cost shock + North American rate collapse (further -15% from current) = shipping operator EBITDA -40 to -60%, triggering covenant breaches on 60% of B-rated issuer debt by H2 2025
- **Base case**: Gradual labour cost pass-through, stable geopolitical risk premium, trans-Pacific rates stabilize at -10% below 2024 average = operator margins compress 200-300bps, leading to selective B-to-CCC migration and reinsurance cost increases of +15-25%
