2. **Refinancing risk**: Carriers with debt maturing 2024-25 may face higher borrowing costs as central banks maintain higher-for-longer rates. Bunker volatility also pressures cash flow reserves.
   - **Action**: Prioritize senior secured / investment-grade rated carriers (Maersk) over unsecured/sub-investment carriers. Review covenant headroom on floating-rate facilities.

#### **B. Reinsurance Cost Outlook (P&C Exposure)**
1. **War risk hardening inevitable**: MSC blast + Red Sea volatility will drive:
   - 15-25% premium increases on hull & liability for Middle East/Red Sea transits (Q2 2024 renewal cycle).
   - Increased attachment points and sub-limits for war risk coverage.
   - Reduced reinsurer appetite for high-accumulation scenarios (multiple vessel transits clustered in high-risk zones).
   
   **Action**: **Increase reinsurance cost assumptions** in underwriting models by 20-30% for marine-related business written pre-Q2 renewals. Model cascading claims scenarios (multiple vessel losses in Strait of Hormuz).

2. **Labour strike tail risk**: UK port strikes + supply-chain rerouting = cargo accumulation at alternative hubs = higher claim frequency for containerized cargo (theft, damage, delay).
   - **Action**: Monitor UK/Northern European port strike calendars; model contingent liability exposure for supply-chain disruption claims (business interruption, contingent cargo).

#### **C. Inflation Assumptions (Fixed Income Modeling)**
1. **Bunker inflation is transitory; rate inflation is persistent**:
   - Do **not** extrapolate bunker spikes into long-term inflation forecasts.
   - **Do** embed 3-5% structural inflation in operational cost bases for capital-intensive maritime services (ports, logistics).
   
   **Action**: Revise long-term inflation assumption for shipping-linked bond yields downward (bunker mean-reversion offsets rate-component inflation). Shorter duration positioning in shipping sector bonds is warranted.

2. **Demand growth is region-specific, not synchronized**:
   - Avoid broad "freight recovery = inflation lift" narratives. Asia-Europe strength does not support transatlantic demand narratives.
   - **Action**: Recalibrate inflation expectations for *traded goods into EMEA* upward (+0.5-1.0 bps), but *US-destined imports* remain soft. Differentiate portfolio inflation hedges by end-market (European vs. North American).
