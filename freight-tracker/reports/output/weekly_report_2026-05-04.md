## (4) ACTIONABLE PORTFOLIO IMPLICATION

### **Recommendation: Recalibrate Shipping & Transport Inflation Assumptions Downward; Increase Duration on Carrier Credit**

**Rationale**:

1. **Inflation persistence is weaker than bunker costs suggest**: The freight rate component (13.1/100) is decoupling from bunker inflation (24.6/100). This indicates that **operator margin compression is absorbing inflationary pressure rather than passing it through to consumers**. Traditional CPI/PPI models that link bunker costs to transport inflation will overstate 2025 transport price growth.
   - Portfolio implication: **Reduce long-term inflation beta** on broad-based inflation hedges (TIPS, long-duration inflation-linked bonds). Shipping costs will not drive headline inflation as powerfully as 2021–2022.

2. **Credit deterioration is structural, not cyclical**: Newbuild capex announcements + incomplete cost pass-through = **margin compression ahead**. Operators like Costamare (leveraged for fleet expansion) and mid-sized lines face refinancing risk in 2025–2026 as debt matures into a higher-rate environment.
   - Portfolio implication: **Extend duration on investment-grade shipping debt** (select carriers with balance-sheet headroom, e.g., MSC private debt, Maersk senior notes) to lock in current coupons before spreads widen on next downcycle. Avoid sub-investment-grade new issuance from overleveraged regional operators.

3. **Reinsurance cost inflation is a real tail risk**: Labour disruptions + bunker volatility will increase **claims frequency** (port delays, damage, cargo loss from diversions) and **claims severity** (longer transits = higher inventory-carrying costs passed to insurers). Regulatory costs (IMO compliance) will elevate operational risk profiles.
   - Portfolio implication: **Prepare for 8–12% premium rate increases on marine/cargo reinsurance** in 2025–2026 renewal seasons, particularly for lines exposed to Mediterranean/European gateway risks.

**Bottom Line**: This market exhibits **inflationary cost pressures not yet visible in rates** (bunker, capex, regulatory). Insurance investment portfolios should expect shipping credit stress to emerge in 2025 while transport inflation surprise to the downside, making selective **duration extension on high-quality shipping debt and reduction in inflation hedges** prudent repositioning moves.
