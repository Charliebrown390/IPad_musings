Shippers unable or unwilling to commit to Hormuz risk are rerouting via Suez to Europe instead, creating artificial demand and rate pressure on long-haul Europe routes. **This is demand elasticity being driven by geopolitical, not macro, factors.**

If Hormuz tensions were creating universal rerouting to long-haul alternatives, Rotterdam rates should spike (Suez-Europe route).
