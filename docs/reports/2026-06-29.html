3. Inflation Assumptions & Reinsurance Cost Outlook

  • Revise embedded freight cost inflation assumptions upward for 2025 in any insurance liability models with supply-chain embedded cost assumptions. Current freight rates (not bunker costs) are the basis for most models; gap the difference.

  • Longer voyage times (increased on-water exposure window) due to Suez rerouting

Expect marine reinsurance premium increases of 10–15% in 2025 renewals, particularly for operators/shippers exposed to Hormuz/Red Sea routes.

4. Hedge Recommendation

  • Long bunker fuel futures (or indirect via shipping index calls) as a hedge for inflation portfolios—bunker fuel is ahead of freight rates and likely to lead into Q2 2025 rate recovery.
  • Short West Coast shipping indices if tactical volatility hedging is desired (cooling momentum will likely accelerate into late Q1).
