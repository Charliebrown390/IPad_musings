Direct Insurance Sector Impact: - P&I clubs will see loss inflation (vessel delays, reroutes via Cape of Good Hope, increased claims frequency) and will demand premium rises of 15–25% for Suez-exposed fleets - Cargo insurers will add geopolitical surcharges or introduce policy limits exclusions for high-risk chokepoints, raising rates for containerized goods shipped Asia-Europe - Reinsurance attached to these risks (XL layers, cat bonds) will become more expensive; reinsurer equity will face margin pressure
