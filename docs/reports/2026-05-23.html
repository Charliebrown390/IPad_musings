Inflation Assumption Adjustment: Update multi-asset inflation models to decouple bunker price inflation from freight rate inflation for 2024. Carriers will absorb cost inflation rather than pass through in soft demand environments—reducing the traditional shipping-to-CPI correlation that fixed-income models typically employ. This implies shipping will not be a reliable inflation hedge in 2024 and should be weighted lower in inflation-hedging allocations.

Conclusion: Freight markets are bifurcated—strong Asia-Europe momentum masking structural North American and transatlantic weakness. Carriers face margin compression; infrastructure operators face revenue upside. Portfolio rebalancing toward the latter, with hedged carrier exposure, best positions insurance investment mandates for 2024's freight market inflection.
